How We Work

From Design To Delivery In 4 Steps

STEP 01

Design & Sampling

Send your artwork or idea. We create a free digital mockup, then a physical sample within 7 days for your approval.

STEP 02

Bulk Production

Sample approved? We start mass production with strict material and stitch control. MOQ from 50 pieces.

STEP 03

Quality Inspection

Every cap passes in-line and final checks for stitching, logo placement, color accuracy and sizing.

STEP 04

Packing & Shipping

Caps are carefully packed and shipped via DHL, FedEx, UPS or sea freight - typical delivery within 15 days.
